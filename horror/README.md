# Horror — research notes

Short design notes for the Far World pilot, gathered from reviews, developer interviews and design write-ups. These are research notes only: nothing here is game code or assets.

Each note opens with a three-sentence summary, then lists the top three games or films in that space with one sentence on why each matters, then links the sources behind the claims.

## Topics

- [Amnesia: The Dark Descent](amnesia-the-dark-descent.md) — sanity as a second health bar, finite light, unkillable monsters, sound-led atmosphere.
- [Resident Evil 7: Biohazard](resident-evil-7.md) — first-person blind spots, flashlight use, scarce ammo, safe rooms and Baker-family domestic dread.
- [Outlast](outlast.md) — no combat, camcorder night vision as rationed sight, roaming pursuers and slow interactions.
- [Escape the Backrooms and Inside the Backrooms](backrooms-games.md) — liminal architecture, exploration pacing, proximity-chat co-op.
- [Dark Souls: Statues, Mannequins and Environmental Storytelling](dark-souls-statues-and-storytelling.md) — Masked Manikins, Belfry Gargoyles, Royal Knight statues, item-text and level-design narrative.
- [Occult and Folk-Horror Visual Language](occult-folk-horror-visual-language.md) — candles, apotropaic sigils, veils, handmade wrongness, liminal domestic spaces.
- [Mannequin Horror](mannequin-horror.md) — why the uncanny human shape works, and the look-doubt-move beat that sells it.
- [Mannequin Video](mannequin-video.md) — drive clip: first snippet of a live recording, a close-up of mannequin horror gameplay; link recorded, not yet reviewed.
- [Condemned: Bart's Department Store — mannequin behaviour](condemned-barts-behaviour-sheet.md) — blending mannequin enemies: what is camera-gated, what is scripted, and why silence is the tell.
- [Moves When Unseen](moves-when-unseen.md) — Hex addendum: SCP-173, the Weeping Angels, FNAF, Silent Hill mannequins, and how "it only moves when you look away" should work in a dark house.
- [Timed Look-Away Reference Sheet](timed-look-away-reference-sheet.md) — Hex brief 3: the four look-away sequences with sourced structure and audio cues, and every timing that still needs measuring.

## Format

Summaries are kept to three sentences so they stay comparable across topics. Sources are listed at the end of each note and cover the specific claims made there; where a claim comes from a wiki entry rather than a design essay, that is noted.
